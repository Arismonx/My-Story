# Code of Conduct

We want My Story to be a friendly place to contribute, for everyone.

## Expected
- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers (Thai and English are both fine in discussions).
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue without personal details or contact the maintainer ([@Arismonx](https://github.com/Arismonx)) through GitHub.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
